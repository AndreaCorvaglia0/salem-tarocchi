## Why each «no», and the one change for each (made in round 2)

| problem | why | change |
|---|---|---|
| tap on a slip: 0/3 | the legend said «toccale» but not what a tap gives | legend: «cose in più: toccane una per leggerla» |
| «Seguila»: 0/3 | the button says the verb, never the consequence | a pencil line under it: «il cerchio rosso si sposta qui»; when she follows it, «La sto seguendo» is text, not a button |
| «7» read as «?» | Caveat's 7 has a curl | small numbers in words on this page («sette»), digits in the story's text set in the serif face |
| faded tick slip | nothing said what it was | a tiny pencil «fatta» under it (still shown once) |
| «2 cose in più»: remaining or total? | | «due cose in più da fare» |
| «autunno» beside a region | the season labels mean nothing here | dropped on this page |
| three slips at one place read as one blob | slips overlapped | slips spaced apart (each its own 44 px tap) and a pencil «tre qui» under them |
| «un ricordo» / «una prova» | vague reward | «Cosa ci guadagno» made concrete: «un oggetto da tenere» instead of «un ricordo» |
