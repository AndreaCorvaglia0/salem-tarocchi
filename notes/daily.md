# Daily texts: facts used (for docs/RESEARCH.md)

Facts behind `src/data/daily/cards.js` (`sguardi` of kind `storia`, some `immagine`) and the history notes in
`src/data/daily/notes.js`. "Checked" = looked up on 16 Nov 2026; the others are common tarot-history knowledge
written from memory, not looked up.

## Checked on the web
- Sola Busca: engraved in the late 15th century (coloured version dated 1491, Ferrara or Venice); the Busca-Serbelloni family gave black-and-white photographs of all 78 cards to the British Museum in 1907; the RWS Three of Swords and Ten of Wands (from the Sola Busca Ten of Swords) echo it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sola_Busca_tarot
- The Hanged Man was called Il Traditore (or L'Impiccato) in 15th-century Italy; traitors were painted in effigy on public walls (shame paintings). The page does not say the paintings showed them hung by one foot: that detail is from memory (pittura infamante). https://tarot-heritage.com/from-trionfi-to-majorarcana/lappeso-le-pendu-the-hanged-man/
- The Visconti-Sforza Hermit is catalogued as "Time" by the Morgan Library (hourglass instead of lantern); search result title only, page not read. https://www.themorgan.org/collection/tarot-cards/time

## From memory, not looked up
- Waite swapped Strength (old XI) and Justice (old VIII) in 1909 so that Strength falls under Leo.
- Tarot de Marseille: Fool unnumbered; Le Bateleur with dice and cups; the Lovers as a youth between two women under Cupid; Chariot drawn by two horses; card XIII often without a name, skeleton on foot with a scythe; La Maison Dieu; Star, Moon (two dogs, crayfish), Sun (two children by a wall), Judgement (flag with cross), World (figure in a wreath, four evangelists) already close to the RWS scenes.
- Visconti-Sforza: Fool in rags with feathers in his hair; Papessa with papal tiara; Empress and Emperor with the imperial eagle; Wheel with regnabo, regno, regnavi, sum sine regno; Temperance without wings; Devil and Tower missing from the surviving deck.
- Chariot sphinxes and the Devil as Baphomet come from Éliphas Lévi (19th century). Hierophant = priest of the Eleusinian mysteries. Judgement called L'Angelo in early Italian lists. Fool numbered 0 by the Golden Dawn (late 19th century).
- Waite, Pictorial Key: Magician's belt is a serpent biting its tail; Empress's diadem has twelve stars; Queen of Swords includes "widowhood".
- Golden Dawn: Empress = Daleth (door); titles Swiftness (8 Wands, Mercury in Sagittarius), Abandoned Success (8 Cups), Peace Restored (2 Swords, Moon in Libra), Harmonious Change (2 Pentacles), Success Unfulfilled (7 Pentacles). Waite and Pamela Colman Smith were both members.
- Suits: Bastoni = clubs/fiori, Coppe = hearts/cuori, Spade = spades/picche, Denari = diamonds/quadri. Tarot born as a trick-taking game (trionfi) in northern Italian courts in the 15th century; divination documented from 18th-century France (Court de Gébelin's Egyptian theory, 1781); RWS published in London by Rider in December 1909; Pamela Colman Smith ("Pixie") was also a stage designer and signed each card with a monogram.
- Calendar notes: weekday planets; Roman month names (Ianus, februare, Mars, Maia, Iuno, Iulius, Augustus, septem..decem); solstices and equinoxes; wheel of the year (Imbolc/Candelora, Beltane/Calendimaggio, Lughnasadh, Samhain, Yule) as a modern name for older feasts; San Giovanni and iperico; cimaruta in Naples.
