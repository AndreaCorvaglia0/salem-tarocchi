# Daily visual data: doubts

`src/data/daily/visual.js` was written by looking at `public/cards/<id>.webp` (350×600) on gridded sheets,
then checked by drawing every look and every `at` back onto the cards. Nothing was tried in the real screen
or on a phone. Cards not listed here looked clear.

  (45,60): the falcon is at the top edge, the snail is cut off.

- m15, m18, m20, w02, w04, c05, s04, s09, p05, p06, p10: the text points at two or three places; the look is

  falling), so the look is a wide central view.
